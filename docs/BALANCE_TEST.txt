FAL-125 BALANCE TEST, every fighter (made for V305, on v303, with the nine types).
How: each fighter played in a team that suits its role. EARLY = 60 runs, World 5 (Freedom at a Price), level 1, Hard (the test that shows differences).
LATE = 24 runs, World 6, level 10, Hard (most fighters win almost every run; noisy: re-running can move a fighter by 4-5 clears).
Teams: Damage with cy, inkating, shamoon. Tank with claire, inkating, shamoon. Healer with sna, snavo, radiant. A fighter already in its team is swapped for a similar one.
Fighters can only be compared inside the same role. Columns: fighter, early wins of 60, early average wave, late wins of 24, late average wave.
The weakest were buffed in V305 (see BUFF305 and the README); this table is BEFORE those buffs.
